Our Story

Rooted in the neighborhood, grown with care.

Bloom & Co. started as a single flower cart at the farmers market. Today we work directly with regional growers to bring you the freshest stems, arranged by hand every morning — never sitting in a warehouse.

  • ✔ Sourced from growers within 150 miles
  • ✔ Arranged fresh the morning of delivery
